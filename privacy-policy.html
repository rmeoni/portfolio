Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit www.uxrodrigo.com (the “Site”), how it's used, and the choices you have. The Site is the personal portfolio of Rodrigo Meoño (“I”, “me”), a UX Developer based in Guatemala. If you have questions, email hello@uxrodrigo.com.

1. Information you give me

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Book a call: your name, email address, optional project details, the date and time you choose, and your time zone.
  • Email: anything you send to hello@uxrodrigo.com.

I use this information only to reply to you, schedule and hold the call you asked for, and follow up about a possible project. I don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Analytics: the home page uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site (for example pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser). Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, or opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Spam protection: the contact and booking forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser to tell people from bots. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy notice.
  • Preferences on your device: the Site saves your light/dark theme and language choice in your browser's local storage so it remembers them next time. On your first visit, the language is chosen from your device's time zone (Spanish for Spain and Latin America, English elsewhere); this check happens in your browser. This stays on your device and isn't sent to me.
  • Server logs: the services that host and deliver the Site (listed below) may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your request, for security and reliability.

3. Service providers

The Site relies on these providers, which process data on my behalf or when your browser loads their content:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site.
  • Cloudflare: Workers that deliver form submissions and bookings, and Turnstile.
  • Google: Analytics; Google Calendar and Google Meet for booked calls; web fonts and scripts loaded from Google servers.
  • Webflow's content delivery network, which serves the jQuery library the Site uses.
  • Vimeo: the embedded masterclass video player, which may set its own cookies when you play a video.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. Some are located outside your country, including in the United States.

4. Booking a call with Google Calendar

When you book a call, an event is created on my Google Calendar with your name, email address, the time you chose and any details you provide, and Google sends you a calendar invitation with a Google Meet link. The booking page only checks when I'm busy. It never asks you to sign in with Google and never accesses your Google account or calendar.

5. How long I keep information

I keep messages and booking details for as long as needed to respond, hold the call and follow up on a project, and for a reasonable period afterwards for my records. Calendar events stay on my calendar until I delete them. Analytics data is kept according to my Google Analytics retention settings. You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@uxrodrigo.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Site still works without them.

7. Security

Form submissions are sent over HTTPS, and access to my calendar is limited to the booking service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Site isn't directed to children under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

10. Contact
